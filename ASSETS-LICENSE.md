This includes:

- `assets/room.jpg`, the room painting
- `assets/sprites/`, Dog's poses and the toys
- `assets/crayon-art.png`, the framed crayon drawing

Dog is a real cat, and her likeness in this artwork is personal. Please don't reuse, redistribute, sell or train models on these files, or use them in other projects, without permission.

You are welcome to play the game, link to it, and fork the repository to learn from or reuse the code. If you build something of your own from the code, replace the assets with your own artwork and music.

## How the assets were made

- The room painting, Dog's poses and the toys were generated with Magnific (using Google's Nano Banana 2 image model) from Daniel's own reference photos.
- The crayon drawing was created with ChatGPT.
- The soundtrack was generated with Suno.

Daniel confirmed his plans with these services allow him to publish the outputs.
